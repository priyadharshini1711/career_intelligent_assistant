Job Title: Senior Backend Engineer, Payments
Company: Halcyon Pay
Location: Remote (India)

ABOUT US
Halcyon Pay processes recurring payments for subscription businesses across
Asia-Pacific. We move about $400M a year. The payments team is nine engineers.

THE ROLE
You will own services on the money path. That means correctness first,
latency second, and a low tolerance for surprises in production.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design and build Python services that handle payment authorisation, capture
  and reconciliation.
- Model financial data in PostgreSQL with a strong bias toward correctness and
  auditability.
- Improve API latency and throughput on endpoints under sustained load.
- Write thorough tests; payments code does not ship without them.
- Participate in design reviews and on-call for the services you own.
- Work with compliance on audit requirements.

REQUIREMENTS
- 4+ years of backend engineering with Python.
- Strong experience with Django or FastAPI in production.
- Deep PostgreSQL knowledge: schema design, indexing, query optimisation.
- Experience building and consuming REST APIs at scale.
- Demonstrated experience with performance optimisation and profiling.
- Solid testing discipline including integration tests.
- Comfortable with Docker and CI/CD pipelines.
- Experience with Redis or a comparable caching layer.

PREFERRED
- Prior work in payments, fintech, or another regulated domain.
- Experience decomposing a monolith into services.
- AWS experience, particularly Lambda and S3.
- Exposure to Kafka or another event streaming platform.
- Experience mentoring engineers.

BENEFITS
Remote-first, annual offsite, equity, and health insurance.
