Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer, Applied AI
Company: Verdant Labs
Location: Bengaluru

ABOUT THE ROLE
We build retrieval-augmented products on top of large language models for
enterprise customers. You would be the fourth engineer on the applied team.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
- Build and ship RAG pipelines: chunking, embedding, retrieval, evaluation.
- Work with vector databases and tune retrieval quality against real queries.
- Design prompts and guardrails for production LLM features.
- Own the evaluation harness and the quality metrics that gate releases.
- Deploy and monitor model-backed services.

REQUIREMENTS
- 3+ years of software engineering, with at least one year on ML or LLM
  systems in production.
- Strong Python.
- Hands-on experience with large language models and prompt engineering.
- Experience with vector databases such as Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant or
  pgvector.
- Familiarity with embedding models and retrieval evaluation.
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Comfortable with Docker and cloud deployment.

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience with LangChain or LlamaIndex.
- Published work, open-source contributions, or conference talks.
- MLOps experience: model serving, monitoring, drift detection.
- Background in NLP or information retrieval.

WHAT WE OFFER
Early-stage equity, a hardware budget, and conference travel.
