Job Title: Senior Platform Engineer
Company: Tessellate Systems
Location: Bengaluru (hybrid, 3 days in office)

ABOUT THE TEAM
Tessellate builds the infrastructure layer that our product engineering teams
deploy onto. We are twelve engineers supporting about ninety. The platform team
owns the deployment pipeline, the service mesh, and the observability stack.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
- Design, build and operate the Kubernetes platform that all product services
  run on.
- Own the CI/CD pipeline end to end and keep deploys under ten minutes.
- Build the observability stack: metrics, structured logging, distributed
  tracing, and the alerting that sits on top of it.
- Write and review Terraform for our AWS estate.
- Partner with product teams on system design reviews and production readiness.
- Take part in a shared on-call rotation.

REQUIREMENTS
- 5+ years of backend or infrastructure engineering.
- Strong Python or Go for tooling and automation.
- Production experience running Kubernetes, not just deploying to it.
- Solid Terraform and infrastructure-as-code discipline.
- Deep AWS knowledge across compute, networking and IAM.
- Experience with Prometheus and Grafana, or an equivalent metrics stack.
- Comfortable with Docker, Linux and CI/CD tooling.
- Track record of debugging production incidents under time pressure.

NICE TO HAVE
- OpenTelemetry or another distributed tracing implementation.
- Service mesh experience (Istio, Linkerd).
- Contributions to open-source infrastructure projects.
- Experience mentoring engineers.

WHAT WE OFFER
Competitive salary, equity, health cover for you and dependants, and a
generous learning budget.
